# FoldStash — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-21 (Asia/Shanghai)
**Contact:** prophet@mail.grokbot.com
**Product:** FoldStash Chrome extension (anonymous publisher)

## Summary

FoldStash is a **local-first** prompt manager. We do **not** operate a server that receives your chat content or prompt library. Payment checkout (when you buy All Access) happens on **Gumroad** **outside** the extension.

## What we store

On your device, in `chrome.storage.local` for your Chrome profile:

- Prompt titles, bodies, tags, folder names, and related timestamps you create
- Extension settings, including a license key string if you activate one

This data stays in the browser profile unless **you** export it or wipe it from Settings.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** upload your ChatGPT (or other) chat transcripts to FoldStash servers
- We do **not** sell personal data
- We do **not** require a FoldStash account to use the free tier
- We do **not** collect analytics in the POC beyond what Chrome itself may show the developer in the Web Store dashboard after publish (if any)

## Permissions (why)

| Permission | Reason |
|------------|--------|
| `storage` | Save your prompt library locally |
| `sidePanel` | Main UI |
| `clipboardWrite` | Copy prompt text on your click |
| `activeTab` | Light page interaction when needed |
| Host `https://chatgpt.com/*` | Show the optional “Save prompt” button |

## Payments

Purchases for **Lifetime All Access ($79)** or optional **$6.99/mo** are processed by **Gumroad** (primary checkout). Their checkout pages and privacy policies govern payment data (card details, receipts). FoldStash receives license entitlement signals you paste into Settings — we do not process card numbers inside the extension.

*(Polar remains a deferred/optional Merchant of Record path and is not used for current checkout.)*

## Third parties

- **OpenAI / ChatGPT website:** Separate service; FoldStash only reads what you choose to save via the content-script affordance in your browser.
- **Gumroad:** Checkout and license key issuance.

## Children

FoldStash is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the Web Store listing or product docs when published.
