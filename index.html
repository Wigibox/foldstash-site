Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21 (Asia/Shanghai) · Contact: prophet@mail.grokbot.com

FoldStash is a local-first prompt manager. We do not operate a server that receives your chat content or prompt library. Payment checkout (when you buy All Access) happens on Gumroad outside the extension.

What we store

On your device, in chrome.storage.local for your Chrome profile:

  • Prompt titles, bodies, tags, folder names, and related timestamps you create
  • Extension settings, including a license key string if you activate one

This data stays in the browser profile unless you export it or wipe it from Settings.

What we do not do

  • Upload your ChatGPT chat transcripts to FoldStash servers
  • Sell personal data
  • Require a FoldStash account for the free tier
  • Collect product analytics in the POC (beyond any Chrome Web Store dashboard metrics after publish)
